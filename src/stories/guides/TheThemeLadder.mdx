# The ladder: two Levels, independently authored

A **Level** is one complete set of colour decisions, selected at runtime by a `data-theme`
attribute. There are two: `midnight` and `sketch`.

## Neither is named for its polarity, and that is the point

`midnight` has polarity `dark`; `sketch` has `light`. Calling them `dark` and `light` would
have collapsed the Level and its polarity into one thing — and then a third Level would need a
new vocabulary rather than a new name.

`midnight` names a ground. `sketch` names a surface and a way of drawing on it. Polarity
survives as a *declared property of* a Level, which is why `dark:` and `light:` variants still
mean something alongside `midnight:` and `sketch:`.

## Neither is derived from the other

This is the expensive decision and it was deliberate. Deriving one palette from another by
inverting lightness produces colours that are arithmetically related and perceptually wrong:
a percentage tuned against near-black does not hold at the light end. `border.subtle` at 28%
is a visible hairline on black and invisible on paper.

So every value is a literal, authored per Level. The cost is twice the authoring; the benefit
is that `pnpm check:contrast` can compute every pair without a browser — **222 pairs across
two Levels** — which is what makes hand-tuned literals sustainable rather than twenty times
the manual review.

## Selecting one

```tsx
<ThemeProvider defaultLevel="midnight">
```

`persist` writes the choice to `localStorage`; `followSystem` reads `prefers-color-scheme`.
Both are on by default, and both are the wrong thing for a capture surface — see
[deterministic rendering](https://github.com/rtkelly13/design-system/blob/main/docs/deterministic-rendering.md),
where `scoped` is the switch that actually does the work.

## Adding a third

Add it to `THEME_LEVELS` and `LEVELS` in `src/theme/levels.ts`, then run `pnpm tokens:build`.

Adding a *role* is a compile error in every Level at once — that is the guarantee the
`Record<ThemeLevel, …>` shape buys, and it is why a Level cannot half-exist. `check:contrast`
will then tell you, in numbers, which of your new pairs do not clear their floor.

Full reasoning: [ADR 0003](https://github.com/rtkelly13/design-system/blob/main/docs/adr/0003-two-levels-independently-authored.md)
and [ADR 0004](https://github.com/rtkelly13/design-system/blob/main/docs/adr/0004-two-axes-level-and-medium.md).
